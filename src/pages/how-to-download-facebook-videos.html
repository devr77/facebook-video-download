How to copy a Facebook video link

Every download starts with the video's URL:

  • Facebook app: tap Share under the video, then Copy link.
  • Browser: click the video's timestamp or open it full screen, then copy the URL from the address bar.
  • Reels: tap Share → Copy link. The link looks like facebook.com/reel/…

All of these formats are supported: facebook.com/watch?v=…, facebook.com/username/videos/…, facebook.com/reel/…, facebook.com/share/v/… and fb.watch/….

How to download Facebook videos on Android

  1. Copy the video link from the Facebook app.
  2. Open {{name}} in Chrome.
  3. Tap Paste, then Download.
  4. Tap Download HD. The video is saved to your Download folder and shows up in Gallery or Google Photos.

How to download Facebook videos on iPhone & iPad

  1. Copy the video link from the Facebook app.
  2. Open {{name}} in Safari (iOS 13 or later).
  3. Paste the link and tap Download, then Download HD. Confirm the download when Safari asks.
  4. Open the Files app → Downloads, tap the video, then Share → Save Video to move it to Photos.

How to download Facebook videos on Windows & Mac

  1. Open the video on facebook.com and copy the URL from the address bar.
  2. Paste it into the box on the {{name}} homepage and click Download.
  3. Click Download HD or Download SD. The MP4 is saved to your Downloads folder.

If the video opens in a new tab instead of downloading, right-click it and choose Save video as…

How to download private Facebook videos

Videos from private groups or profiles can't be fetched from a link because they need your login. Use the Private Video Downloader instead: open the video on a computer, press Ctrl+U, copy the whole page source and paste it into the tool. Everything is processed in your browser.

Troubleshooting

  • "No downloadable video found": the video is private, deleted, or the link points to a post rather than the video. Open the video itself and copy that link.
  • Only SD is available: the uploader didn't publish an HD version.
  • The link expired: Facebook's download links expire after a few hours. Paste the video URL again to get fresh links.
  • Nothing happens on iPhone: make sure you're using Safari, not the in-app Facebook browser.
