About {{name}}

{{name}} is a free web tool for saving Facebook videos to watch offline. We built it to be fast, simple and respectful of your privacy, with no sign-ups, no pop-up ads and no software to install.

How it works

When you paste a link, our server reads the public Facebook page and finds the video file addresses Facebook already provides to web browsers. We then give you those links. Videos are streamed from Facebook's servers and we never host or store them.

Use responsibly

Please respect creators. Only download videos you own, or that you have permission to save, and don't re-upload other people's content. {{name}} is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Facebook or Meta Platforms, Inc.
